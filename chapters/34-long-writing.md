> As a human even if we chase success and keep on running at some point of time our heart and our soul stops wanting for a time of peace to rest and feel life. And when we get overburdened by the despondence, success load, and materialistic world, our heart starts seeking Peace and we find it in lap of nature. Where our internet connectivity and reel world end, we get to to see the beauties that heaven poured on us. In search of heaven we forget that heaven actually resides in this earth, it is our incapability that we dont take out our time to recognise the heaven on this earth.

>After a long working year and immense pressure of exams, inorder to free my shoulder from those load and lift up my soul I started searching for a nearby tourist spot and the one that just popped on my mind was Sikkim. It was 8th june midnight 3:30 am and not even my uncle sun had rose from his sleep, but my eyes were already wide open, as my excitement was out of its limit and thus my eyes couldn't even close itself. And finally after a 9 hours of journey I finally landed to NJP.
[br]
>From their the journey started and I must say that I was lucky enough to get a window side seat. In the beginning the quick turning roads were dizzing me off, but as days passed I gradually got habituated to it. The bright, dark-green leaves glowed with a soft, baby tender hue whenever the sunlight touched them. Sun rays slipped through the canopy, illuminating the leaves making them shimmer against the deeper greens of the forest. And along the way stood dark-brown woods, covered in patches of velvety green moss. Tiny droplets of crystal-clear water gathered on the leaves and branches, tinkled gently down through the woods. The coolness of the surroundings, the faint sound of water, and the earthy scent of the moist soil and woods made everything feel wonderfully untouched.[br]
>As we gradually moved up the mountains, with each turns Soft, cotton-like fog began obstructing our path, slowly swallowing the distant mountains and trees. It felt as though our car had entered a whole new world of wonders, something I had never experienced before. The black road stretched ahead of us, glistening faintly as our car glided smoothly through the mist.
>Soon, I was completely encircled by clouds. Wisps of fog drifted past the windows, carrying tiny droplets of water that settled upon my face. I could almost feel the mist like a gentle touch from heaven, a kiss of care and coolness upon my skin. Everything had blended into one mesmerizing dream.
[br]
>Passing through Rangpo, I first time got to see River Teesta. From my childhood, I only saw the calm and steady figure of River Ganga, but first time I saw a river with such a violent nature, its crystal bluish green water was smashing the rocks beneath it. The dazzling white waves looked like white sparkling diamond stiched on the royal saree worn by river Teesta. It looks so beautiful when it is calm, but their violence can also make them a devastating beauty. So I feel, we should take care of our nature, if her love can bring heaven on earth, then her anger can also burn us into ashes too.[br]
