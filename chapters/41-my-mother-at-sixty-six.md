## Understanding The Text ##

**1. Driving from my parent’s**
[br]
**home to Cochin last Friday** 
[br]
**morning, I saw my mother,**
[br]
**beside me,**
[br]
**doze, open mouthed, , her face**
[br]
**ashen like that**
[br]
**of a corpse and realised with pain**
[br]
**that she was as old as she looked**
[br]
Ans: When the Author was driving back from her Parent's home to Cochin, She saw her mother sleeping beside her with mouth open. Her face was had became pale and thus the pale face of her is being compared to the pale ash color of a corpse. Dozing of her mother with mouth open symbolize restlessness and lack of energy. Her pale face reflects lack of warmth, energy, vividness and life. 
[br]She silently realised with pain that Her mother has grown old, and she might loose her.
[br]
[br]
**2. but soon**
[br]
**put that thought away, and** 
[br]
**looked out at Young** 
[br]
**Trees sprinting, the merry children spilling**
[br]
**out of their homes**
[br]
Ans: Inorder to drive her attention away from such painful thought and fear she looked outside through the window and she saw a contrasting image outside the Car. Young Trees growing rapidly and Small childrens are happily coming out of their house. These Young green trees symbolizes freshness, Energy, and life. 
Whearas the image of happy childrens spilling out of their house symbolizes warmth, joy, energy and happiness.
[br]
[br]
**3. but after the airport’s**
[br]
**security check, standing a few yards**
[br]
**away, I looked again at her, wan, pale**
[br]
**as a late winter’s moon and felt that old**
[br]
**familiar ache, my childhood’s fear**
[br]
Ans: After the airport's security check when Author again looked at her mother's wan, pale face she felt a old familar ache, her childhood fear that she might loose her mother. The old familar ache of seperation striked her again.
[br]
The wan, pale face of her mother is being compared to late winter's moon. Winter night is mostly reflected as silent and lifeless. In winter the trees looses their leaves and their vivid green color, everything becomes frozen, cold, pale and dry. Thus She used this phare to symbolize her mother's present state of  lifelessness, lack of joy, warmth and energy.
[br]
[br]

**4. but all I said was, see you soon, Amma,[br]**
**all I did was smile and smile and smile......[br]**
Before leaving the only thing that she told to her mom was that she will meet her Soon. Her parting words signify that although she silently realized that her mother has grown old and she might loose her soon, the time for seperation is nearby, but still she had a hope in her heart that she will again meet with her very soon. 
[br] And then she smiled and Smiled. Her continuous smile reflects that she tried to hide her pain ,sorrow and her childhood fear of seperation by a veil of smile. She tried to comfort herself and also wanted to leave with a positive hope that she will meet with her again.
