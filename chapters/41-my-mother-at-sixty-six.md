ANS: When the Author was driving back from her Parent's home to Cochin, She saw her mother sleeping beside her with mouth open. The wan, pale face of her mother is being compared to late winter's moon. Winter night is mostly reflected as silent and lifeless. In winter the trees looses their leaves and their vivid green color, everything becomes frozen, cold, pale and dry. [br] And then she smiled and Smiled. Her continuous smile reflects that she tried to hide her pain ,sorrow and her childhood fear of seperation by a veil of smile. She tried to comfort herself and also wanted to leave with a positive hope that she will meet with her again.

[Q]1. What is the kind of pain and ache that the poet feels?[/Q]
[br]
[br]

Ans: The poet had a similar pain and ache that she used to have durig her childhood, the fear of seperation from her mother. During her way back to cochin from her Parent's home, when she looked at her mother she silently understood with pain that her mother has grown old, and she might loose her mother soon. The fear of seperation, death and detachment from her mother is the pain that the poet felt.

[Q]2. Why are young trees described as ‘sprinting’?[/Q]
[br]
[br]

A: Young trees are green and these represents their freshness, vividness, and presence of life and warmth. And by Young trees 'Sprinting' poet wanted to say that the trees growing rapidly. To symbolize the vibrant energy and growth present inside them.
[br]
[br]

[Q]3. Why has the poet brought in the image of the merry children ‘spilling out of their homes’?[/Q]
[br]
[br]

Ans: The poet during her way back to cochin from her Parent's home saw that her Mother has grown really old enough and she has fully lost her energy, her warmth and the poet felt that she might loose her mother soon. And to drive away this thought she looked outside of the window, and then she portrays a completely contrasting image that she saw outside. She saw small childrens happily coming out of their homes. The happy childrens here symbolize happiness, joy, warmth, and energy. She used the phrase to contrast it to her mother's present state.

[Q]4. Why has the mother been compared to the ‘late winter’s moon’?[/Q]
[br]
[br]

Ans: In late Winter's moon, the pale moon reflects Poet's mother's wan, pale face. During this time trees looses their vivid green color and leaves, symbolising loss of youth, freshness and vividness. The cold, frozen winter reflects lack of warmth, joy, and life.
Her mother has also grown old and her wan,pale face is compared to a frozen late winter's moon inorder to symbolise the lack of energy, joy, happiness, vividness, youth, freshness and warmth

[Q]5. What do the parting words of the poet and her smile signify?[/Q]
[br]
[br]

Ans: While driving back to cochin, during her way the Poet had silently realised that her mother has grown really old enough and her childhood fear that she may lose her mother will become true, but still before leaving her mother at the airport she had a positive hope that she will again meet her soon. Her words signify her love for her Mother and her desire to see her mother again. 
[br]
But her smile symbolizes her inner pain and fear of Seperation, that she tried to hide with a veil of Smile. It suggest that she had understood the fate and was trying to silently accept nature's  cruel process of life and death. Her smile also suggest that shewanted to leave with a hope of seeing her again. Her inner grief, sadness, and pain got covered up by her continuous smile.
