## Justification of the Title
In the story, the author reflects on how the remains of King Tut were found by the archaeologists, the traditions of the people of ancient Egypt, and the details of the investigations done by Howard Carter, who discovered the Pharaoh's grave for the first time. [br] The story also states that King Tut's remains were subjected to X-ray scans and CT scans to find out the mysteries of the life and death of King Tut. So the author not only talked about discovering Tut by finding his remains from the Egyptian Valley of Kings but also talked about how people tried many modern forensic methods to discover other possible mysteries regarding the life of Tut and the reason behind his untimely death. So the title says 'Discovering Tut'. [br] Also, since they are still discovering and unfolding many mysteries, thus they say that the saga, or the story, of the King continues.

## NCERT Questions Solution:
[Q]QUESTION 1: Give reason:[br]
(i) King Tut's body had been subjected to repeated scrutiny.[/Q][br]

ANS: King Tut had died at a very early age. Thus, his body had been subjected to repeated scrutiny to find the reason and mysteries of his untimely death.[br]

[Q] (ii) Howard Carter's investigation was resented.[/Q][br]

ANS: When Howard Carter and his men found the remains of King Tut for the first time, they ran into a problem because the resins that were used during the burial rituals had hardened, cementing Tut to the bottom of the gold coffin. Even by heating it to 149 degrees Fahrenheit for several hours, he could not separate the mummy, so they had to chisel away the consolidated material from beneath the limbs. [br]
Also, to protect it from thieves, they had to separate the mummy from his adornments, and in the process of doing so, they removed the mummy's head and disconnected almost all the major joints. And this had caused huge tampering with the actual remains of King Tut. This might have destroyed many evidences that could have revealed the mysteries of the life and death of King Tut. So his investigation was resented.[br]

[Q] (iii) Carter had to chisel away the solidified remains to raise the King's remains[/Q][br]

ANS: The resins that were used during the burial ceremony had dried up, cementing the remains of Tut's body to the bottom of the coffin, and by any measures and any amount of force, he could not detach the mummy from the resins, so he had to chisel away the solidified consolidated resin from beneath the limbs and trunk to detach the mummy from the coffin.[br]

[Q] (iv) Tut's body was buried along with gilded treasures[/Q][br]

ANS: During the time of ancient Egypt, the royals used to think or hope that they would be able to take their riches along with them. So King Tut was buried along with gilded treasures as they believed that in this way he would be able to take it for his afterlife or his journey after death.[br]

[Q] (v) The boy king changed his name from Tutankhaten to Tutankhamun[/Q][br]

ANS: King Tut wanted to restore and bring back the old principles and practices and traditions as followed by Amun. He was inspired by him and thus changed his name to Tutankhamun, which means a living image of Amun.[br]

[Q]QUESTION 2:[br] (i) List the deeds that led Ray Johnson to describe Akhenaten as wacky[/Q][br]

ANS: Akhenaten promoted the worship of Aten, and changed his name to Akhenaten, which means the servant of Aten. He then shifted the religious capital from Thebes to Akhetaten. Not only this, he had also attacked a major god, Amun, smashed his images, and closed his temples. All these activities were something unusual, horrific, and absurd. And that is why he said that "Akhenaten went a little wacky".[br]

[Q] (ii) What were the results of the CT scan?[/Q][br]

ANS: After the CT scan, 1700 digital X-ray images were produced in cross section. And from these images, a three-dimensional figure of King Tut was generated. A grey-coloured three-dimensional representation of his head, neck vertebrae, hands, rib cage, and a transection of his skull was formed from the scan.[br]

[Q] (iii) List the advances in technology that have improved forensic analysis.[/Q]

ANS: Modern forensics has started using many modern technologies like X-rays and CT scan machines to find out the intricate details of the person, and also any traces of weapon attacks that affected the bones. They use carbon dating to find out the time of death, DNA testing and fingerprinting technology to find blood relations. Not only these, they use modern computer algorithms and database searching, Artificial Intelligence, and chemical testing to find out various mysteries behind the death of a person.[br]

[Q] (iv) Explain the statement "King Tut is one of the first mummies to be scanned - in life and death"[/Q]

ANS: There were no CT scan machines during the time of the ancient Egyptians. So none of the mummies had ever been scanned when they were alive. [br]Also, King Tut was one of the first mummies to be scanned using a CT scan machine, thus they said that he is also one of the first to be scanned in death.
