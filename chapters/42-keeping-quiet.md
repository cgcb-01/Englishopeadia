## Understanding the text 
[Q]1. A thing of beauty is a joy forever[br]
Its loneliness increases and it will never [br]
pass into nothingness[/Q]
[br]

Ans: The poet wants to say that when they see a beautiful thing they remember it forever and its loveliness increases or it becomes more precious with time. It never fades nor its value or importance decreases.
[br]
[br]

[Q]2. But will keep a bower quiet for us[/Q]
[br]

Ans: Bower means a pleasant shady place or a shelter.The poet wants to say that a beautiful memory will be like a presence of a pleasant quiet shelter that will bring happiness and calmness to the person
[br]
[br]

[Q]3. And a sleep full of sweet dreams and health and Quiet breathing[/Q]
[br]

Ans: Poet wants to say that all the beautiful things that we see in this world brings happiness and a sleep full of sweet dreams and from our happy ad peaceful mind comes good health and thus quiet breathing symbolising peace.
[br]
[br]

[Q]4. Therefore on every morrow are we wreathing 
[br]a flowery band to bind us to the searth[/Q]
[br]

Ans: The poet wants to say that because of these beautiful things on this earth every morning we are weaving a flowery band that binds or holds us to this earth
[br]
[br]

[[split]]

[Q]5. Spite of despondence, of the inhuman dearth[br]
Of noble natures, of the gloomy days,[br]
Of all the unhealthy and o’er-darkened ways[/Q]
[br]

Ans: Even though this earth is full of sadness, sorrow,hopelessness , cruelty, darkness and lack of warmth, generosity among characters. It is because of the beautiful things that the nature presents to us that every morning we bind ourselves to this by a flowery band.
[br]
[br]

[Q]6.Some shape of beauty moves away the pall [br]
from our dark spirits [/Q]
[br]

Ans: The pall represents a covering of despondence, suffering, sadness and depression that covers a dark spririts which is full of evil feelings of human beings like Envy, cruelty, selfishness and Malice. Poet wants to say that even if we are encircled by the darkness, but the beautiful things that the nature presents to us and the grandeour of noble people helps to lift this pall of despondence from our dark spririts.
