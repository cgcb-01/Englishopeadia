**Writing skills : Notice Writing**

## Format of the Notice

1. Write Inside a box. Mention the information that are needed.
[br]For Example:[br]
    a. [B] Event: [/B] is being Organised include the context of the event, the date, time, venue, who are being welcomed, and the chief guest (if there).
    [br]
    b. [B]Competition: [/B] include the details of the event, judge, date and time and venue, Prize and the registration process.
    [br]
    c.  [B]School tour: [/B] Here you need to include destination, food and travel service provided, charges taken from the students, classes allowed, Which teachers will be there for guidance and conduction. Registration timing.
    [br]
    d. Health Checkup Drive: 
    [br]
    e. Lost and Found: 
    [br]

# Example of Format: 

>[C]Organisation name (ABC School, Delhi)[/C]
>**NOTICE**
>Date in expanded format (Date Month, year)
>[C][U]A CATCHY HEADING[/U][/C]
>Notice body[br]
>Include the Event details you can write it in parah or in points under event details[br]
[br]
>Name[br]
>Post
