>Why do you think a distance arouse between the author and his grandmother when they moved to the city?[br]
>when they both went to the city there author started to go to an english medium school. He was no longer being taught about god and scriptures but about english, western sciecnce like law of gravity, archimedes principle, the world is round, etc. His grandmother did not believe in these nor she was able to understand the english words that he used to use. Also she was not accompanying him for his school, nor she was able to help him with his studies. These conflicts in thoughts had started creating a gap. But one day author told his grandmother that they were being given music classes in their school. She disapproved it as according to her music has a lewd association .It is the monopoly of harlots and beggars and not for gentle folk. And after this she rarely talked with the author. It was mainly her disbelief regarding the things taught in the school, and her strong disapproval that created a distance between them.
