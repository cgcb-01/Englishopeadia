>The poet asked to the rain who are you, and the rain answered that it is a poem written by the earth. It rises high towards eternal in a way that it cannot be perceived by us. It rises out of the land and bottomless sea towards the eternity in a way that it cannot be perceived by us. [br]It moves upward towards the heaven and there it altogether gets formed into an undefinite shape. It completely gets changed but still remains the same. Here poet writes this to say that the water vapour that rises upwards from the lands and seas, gets transformed into clouds, of an undefinite shape. [br]By the word completely gets changed but still remains the same, the poet writes this to say that the water vapour that rises from the land and deep sea gets transformed from gaseous to liquid state, so it changes the state but remains the same.
>[br] Also the rain says that it descends down to remove drought and wash out dust layers. Without rain the seeds would remain unborn and latent as the rain provides the water needed for the germination. Thus we can say that the rain is originated from the lands and seas on this earth and after falling it helps in sustaining life on this earth. So it says that it gives life to its own origin.

>[Q]1. What are the two voices in the poem?[/Q][br]

>ANS: The two voices in the poem are those of the poet and rain.[br]

>[Q]2. Why "it is strange to tell"?[/Q][br]
>ANS: It is strange to tell because here poet says that the falling rain gave an answer to the question when he said that who art thou? Poet says that the rain itself said about itself. Since it is not a human so it cannot speak and say about itself. Thus he says that it is strange to tell.[br]

>[Q]3. Why the rain says that it is impalpable when it rises?[/Q][br]

>ANS: The water from the land and sea rises in the form of water vapour. Impalpable means it cannot be touched or seen. The rain when rises as water vapour it cannot be touched nor seen . So it says itself as impalpable[br]

>[Q]4. Why the rain says itself as the poem of the Earth?[/Q][br]

>ANS: The rain has a soft rhythmic sound when it falls on the earth. As poem carries happiness, and emotions similarly rain brings life, happiness, and emotions on earth. So it is called as the poem of the earth.[br]

>[Q]5. How the rain describes it's life. Does it corresponds to what you studied in science?[/Q][br]

>ANS: In the poem the rain says that it rises from sea and land high above and there gets transformed into an indefinite shape, changes its form altogether and then descends down as rain on earth.[br]
>On the other hand the science tells that water from land and sea rises upward as water vapour there it condenses to form water droplets combines to form clouds of indefinite shape and then falls down as rain. Thus the poem rightly corresponds and aligns itself to what it is being stated by science.[br]
