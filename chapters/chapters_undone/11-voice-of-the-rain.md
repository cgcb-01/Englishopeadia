>The poet asked to the rain who are you, and the rain answered that it is a poem written by the earth. It rises high towards eternal in a way that it cannot be perceived by us. It rises out of the land and bottomless sea towards the eternity in a way that it cannot be perceived by us. [br]It moves upward towards the heaven and there it altogether gets formed into an undefinite shape. It completely gets changed but still remains the same. Here poet writes this to say that the water vapour that rises upwards from the lands and seas, gets transformed into clouds, of an undefinite shape. [br]By the word completely gets changed but still remains the same, the poet writes this to say that the water vapour that rises from the land and deep sea gets transformed from gaseous to liquid state, so it changes the state but remains the same.
>[br] Also the rain says that it descends down to remove drought and wash out dust layers. Without rain the seeds would remain unborn and latent as the rain provides the water needed for the germination. Thus we can say that the rain is originated from the lands and seas on this earth and after falling it helps in sustaining life on this earth. So it says that it gives life to its own origin.
